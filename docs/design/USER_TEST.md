# Owner-run direction comparison — UNVERIFIED

No participant research was performed by the coding agent. This is a protocol,
not a result. Recruit five consenting owners: at least two less-technical and
two frequent keyboard users. Include an accessibility need where participants
volunteer it; do not collect diagnoses. Use synthetic local study documents,
not personal conversations, and never upload recordings or answers.

Before testing, ensure real backends for each chosen task work. Unavailable
capabilities are excluded and recorded, not represented by fake product data.
Keep Hermes as the comparison and counterbalance the three new directions
across participants. Five people cannot perfectly balance every order; record
the order and treat findings as qualitative, not statistical superiority.

## Session1: two hours with breaks allowed

1. Explain local recording/notes consent; no recording by default. Record OS,
   viewport, font scale, density, input method and theme order without identity.
2. Establish a Hermes baseline: ask locally, read a long answer, inspect a real
   citation, copy code, stop a generation and recover from an actual error.
3. Rotate through Paper,Night Study,Studio using the same successful tasks.
   Include dark/light, keyboard-only navigation, mobile or200% zoom, scrolling
   up during streaming and returning to latest. Do not coach until blocked.
4. Let the participant choose a pack for sustained reading/work. Record time
   spent per theme and breaks; a short exposure cannot establish two-hour comfort.
5. Note wrong clicks, missed sources, lost focus, unexpected scroll, glare,
   eye strain, confusion and unavailable functionality. Record actual elapsed
   times/clicks; never substitute an observer's estimate.

## Session2:30 minutes on a later day

Repeat unassisted theme/density selection, history recovery, cited reading,
keyboard discovery and error recovery. Ask what they remember and what they
changed. Keep task content and configuration stable except deliberate tests.

## Questionnaire after each direction

Rate1-5: reading ease, scanning, visual calm, source visibility, control clarity,
keyboard discoverability, comfort. Ask: What felt hard to read? What distracted
you? Which action was unclear? Did privacy wording imply more than you observed?
Which direction would you choose for reading versus repeated tasks, and why?
Would you change density/font size first? What would stop you using it again?

Report anonymized observations, exposure duration, order, blockers, task success
and exact measurements. Retain consent locally; delete study data on request.
Do not infer "best", accessibility certification or long-session comfort from
five preferences. Add findings to CRITIQUE.md and the default REVIEW_QUEUE entry;
the owner still explicitly chooses whether the shipped default changes.
